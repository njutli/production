# Community Claim

## What can be stated

On official JuiceFS main commit 53835e2481f45cba159cdbcc1ce0f1fc576e3f1a
(2026-08-17), the target behavior is deterministically reproducible:

- A full block written before the asynchronous slice ID becomes ready is not
  dispatched (FlushTo) after the ID is set. This is an ordering issue caused
  by the mutex-protected prepareID goroutine, not a data race.
- The B candidate patch adds a catch-up FlushTo after SetID in prepareID,
  conditioned on positive ID, non-frozen state, and slen >= blockSize.
  FlushTo errors are mapped to syscall.EIO.
- The patch modifies only pkg/vfs/writer.go (7 lines) and adds
  pkg/vfs/writer_flush_test.go (3 regression tests).
- B passes all three tests, count=100, race=20, full pkg/vfs, gofmt, vet,
  and standard Linux build.
- The two-file patch applies cleanly via standard `git apply` on a fresh
  clone and produces identical results.
